A one-page newspaper front page that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in a
single image — 41 stories, drawn, not photographed.

The front page leads with the day's three biggest stories: the Asian market
rout (Nikkei -5.06%, Kospi -5.21%), the International Court of Justice
ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, and the Houthi
missile strike on Riyadh's tallest tower. Below them, ten illustrated cards
cover Wall Street's slide into correction, Anthropic's second-day fall,
Cyclone Akvan's landfall, GTA VI's record $3.79 billion launch day and more,
followed by 28 one-line briefs from every region, a dotted world map and a
numbers strip.

Every story was checked for its date: all 41 happened or were first reported
on 20 November 2026. Three items that were announced on Thursday and first
published on Friday are flagged as such in the red DATE CHECK box — the
Pete Rose pardon (19 November) is not in this paper at all.

Built with Pillow from a single generator script (`generate.py`); every
picture on the page is an original drawing. `main.py` copies the pre-built
`entry.png` to the output directory.
